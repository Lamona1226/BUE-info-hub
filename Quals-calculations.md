GPA Calculation
8 and 9 Subjects

The total of the 8 selected subjects × the total maximum GPA available (40) ÷ the total available grade from all subjects (800). 

Example: 720 x 40 ÷ 800 = 36%. 

Same calculation for the 9 subjects, just change 800 to 900. Example: 820 x 40 ÷ 900 = 36.4%. The total grade of subjects has changed from 720 to 820 as we have selected an additional subject. 

To summarise, we add up the EST 1 + EST 2 (if taken) + GPA… 40.5 + 9 + 36 = 85.5%

Then calculate the total of grades achieved x 20 ÷ 560
